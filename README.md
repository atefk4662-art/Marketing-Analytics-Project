# Marketing-Analytics-Project
This project addresses a business challenge faced by ShopEasy, an online retail business. Despite high marketing investments, the company experienced reduced customer engagement and declining conversion rates. The goal of this analysis is to identify areas for improvement and optimize marketing strategies using data-driven insights.
 Business Problems

Reduced Engagement: A decline in customer interactions with the site and marketing content.  


Decreased Conversion: Fewer visitors are transitioning into paying customers.  


High Marketing Expenses: Significant investments are not yielding the expected ROI.  
+1


Need for Feedback Analysis: A requirement to understand customer sentiment to guide improvements.  

 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Conversion Rate: 8.5% (Average).  
+1


Social Media Views: ~2.98 Million total views.  
+1


Click-Through Rate (CTR): 15.37%.  
+2


Customer Rating: 3.7 / 5.0 (Target: 4.0).  

 Key Insights
1. Conversion Trends

Seasonal Peaks: January saw the highest conversion at 18.5%, largely driven by Ski Boots (150% conversion rate).  


Low Performance: May hit a low of 4.3%, indicating a need for strategic promotions during this period.  


Top Products: Kayaks, Ski Boots, and Baseball Gloves are the highest-converting categories.  

2. Customer Engagement

Content Performance: Blog content drove the most views, particularly in April and July.  


Declining Engagement: Social media views began a steady decline starting in August.  


Interaction Gap: While clicks and likes are low relative to views, the high CTR suggests that engaged users interact effectively.  
+1

3. Sentiment Analysis

Positive Base: 275 reviews show positive sentiment, with the majority being 4 and 5-star ratings.  
+1


Optimization Potential: 82 negative reviews and several "Mixed" sentiments highlight specific areas for product and service improvements.  
+1

 Strategic Recommendations

Targeted Marketing: Focus budget on high-performing categories like Kayaks and Ski Boots during peak months.  


Content Revitalization: Experiment with interactive videos and user-generated content to reverse the decline in social media engagement.  


Feedback Loop: Analyze mixed and negative reviews to identify recurring issues and follow up with dissatisfied customers to improve the average rating to the 4.0 target.
